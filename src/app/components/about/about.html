About Adam

Over the past year I've built a strong foundation in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, Angular, SQL, and Git — with a particular focus on frontend development and building with Angular. I enjoy crafting clean, user-focused web experience and sweating the details that make things feel right for the people using them. You can check out some of that work in my Projects section or on my GitHub.
